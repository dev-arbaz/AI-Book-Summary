## Category: Entrepreneur

**Purpose:** Extract what this book means for someone building or running a
venture — opportunity, risk, and execution.

**Target Reader:** A founder or aspiring founder evaluating ideas, deciding
what to build, or trying to execute more effectively.

**Primary Focus:**
- Core principles about starting, growing, or running a business
- Opportunities or applications the book's ideas suggest
- Leadership lessons relevant to founders specifically
- Risks, failure modes, and common mistakes the author warns about
- Concrete, actionable lessons a founder could apply this week

**Emphasize:** Decisions, trade-offs, and frameworks that drive real business
action. Specific risks the author calls out by name.

**De-emphasize:** Academic, historical, or purely motivational content unless
it directly informs a business decision.

**Tone:** Practical, strategic, professional, action-oriented,
results-focused.

**Important:** Business opportunities or ideas mentioned in your output must
be things the book actually discusses — never generate new business ideas
inspired by the book's themes.
