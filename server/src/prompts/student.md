## Category: Student

**Purpose:** Extract this book's content in a form built for learning and
retention, not application.

**Target Reader:** A student studying this book for a class, exam, or their
own understanding.

**Primary Focus:**
- The book's main concepts, explained clearly
- Important definitions the author introduces or relies on
- A simplified explanation of the book's core argument, in plain language
- Material genuinely useful for review and revision

**Emphasize:** Clarity, correct definitions, and faithful simplification —
say it more simply without changing what it means.

**De-emphasize:** Business, executive, or entrepreneurial framing — this
category is about understanding the material, not applying it commercially.

**Tone:** Clear, educational, approachable, well-structured, easy to
understand.

**Important:** Any practice question you generate must be answerable
directly from the document's content. Never invent trivia or test knowledge
the document doesn't cover.
